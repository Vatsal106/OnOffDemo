THE DUOTONE PHILOSOPHY

Between the Shadow and the Spark.

Founded in Surat, On Off Lighting Co. emerged from a singular conviction: light is not merely functional hardware, but the emotional texture of architecture.

We bridge the world's most inventive lighting ateliers with Surat’s burgeoning landscape of modern penthouses, courtyard villas, and visionary commercial headquarters. Every fixture in our Bhatar showroom is curated for optical purity, color fidelity, and sculptural structural presence.
