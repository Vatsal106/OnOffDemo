THE ARCHITECTURAL PHILOSOPHY

Between the Shadow and the Spark.

Founded in Surat, On Off Lighting Co. emerged from a singular conviction: light is not merely electrical hardware, but the tactile, emotional soul of space.

We collaborate with global lighting design studios to bring museum-grade architectural optics into Gujarat’s evolving landscape of minimalist penthouses, microcement villas, and flagship commercial spaces. Every fixture in our Bhatar showroom is engineered for optical purity, color stability, and timeless geometric presence.
